<h2>How I Approach Sustainability Work</h2>

<p>I approach sustainability problems from both a research and practical perspective.</p>

<p>I start by understanding the problem, identifying the material sustainability issues, examining the available evidence and data, and then translating the findings into clear, actionable insights.</p>

<p>My goal is not simply to identify that a sustainability problem exists, but to understand its causes, measure its significance, and communicate what the evidence means for decision-making.</p>
