# Adepeju Babalola | Sustainability Portfolio

Welcome. I’m Adepeju Babalola, a sustainability and environmental management researcher working at the intersection of climate resilience, sustainability analysis, data, and environmental health.

This portfolio brings together selected sustainability projects, research outputs, analyses, and practical work I have completed across different sustainability contexts.

## About Me

I have an academic background in Environmental Management, with research interests in climate resilience, environmental health, informal systems, community resilience, circular economy, sustainability reporting, and ESG.

I am particularly interested in using research, data, and sustainability frameworks to understand environmental problems and translate findings into practical solutions.

Alongside my academic research, I have been developing practical sustainability and ESG skills through independent projects, professional learning, and applied case studies.

## How I Approach Sustainability Work

I approach sustainability problems from both a research and practical perspective.

I start by understanding the problem, identifying the material sustainability issues, examining the available evidence and data, and then translating the findings into clear, actionable insights.

My goal is not simply to identify that a sustainability problem exists, but to understand its causes, measure its significance, and communicate what the evidence means for decision-making.

## Beyond the Portfolio

My broader research interests focus on climate resilience and environmental health, particularly among vulnerable and underserved populations.

I am interested in how environmental risks, social vulnerability, informal systems, and institutional responses interact — and how better evidence can support more resilient and sustainable communities.
