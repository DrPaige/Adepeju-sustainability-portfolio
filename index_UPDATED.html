Profile

I am an environmental management researcher building practical experience in sustainability. My work brings together environmental research, sustainability analysis, ESG assessment, environmental performance, life-cycle thinking, reporting and data analysis.

I have developed practical experience through virtual sustainability projects and independent environmental projects, while also drawing on previous experience as a Business Analyst in a software development company, where I elicited requirements, coordinated projects and communicated with business and technical stakeholders.

I am particularly interested in the space where environmental sustainability, data, business decisions and social impact meet.
